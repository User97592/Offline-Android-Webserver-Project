The Moto e5 play was released in July 2018. Its specs are below.

Operating System: Android 8.0 (Oreo) / Oreo Go edition

Memory (RAM): 2 GB

Battery: 2800 mAh with 10W rapid charger (Micro-USB)

Front Camera: 5 MP with selfie flash/light
Physical Dimensions: 151 mm x 74 mm x 8.85 mm (5.94" x 2.91" x 0.34")
Weight: 150 g (5.29 oz)
Connectivity: 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n, Bluetooth 4.2 LE, 3.5 mm headset jack, FM radio (no NFC)
Sensors: Fingerprint reader, proximity, accelerometer, ambient light, magnetometer
